## About Us

**Vertex UoM** is a team of Batch 24 students from the IT & Management (ITM) degree programme at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.

We collaborate to turn ideas into practical software solutions, applying our knowledge of software engineering, system design, and modern technologies to real-world problems.

Through our projects, we aim to build reliable, maintainable software while growing together as engineers.

## Our Project

### Sanchara

Sanchara is a multi-tenant platform designed for independent accommodation businesses in Sri Lanka. It helps businesses manage their properties and accept direct reservations through their own websites.

Our goal is to help smaller accommodation providers manage their operations and establish a stronger direct booking presence.

**Key areas of focus**

- Property and accommodation management
- Direct reservation management
- Multi-tenant architecture
- Modern web application development
